Methods & data

What angle sends the ball through the hoop?

Bisection and safeguarded Newton find angles where the predicted center of the ball reaches rim height on its way down, using y(xrim; θ) − 3.05 = 0.

Try both angles in the game. A flatter shot can hit the rim because the whole ball has to fit through the opening.

How much does the time step matter?

Euler uses the slope at the start of each step. Runge–Kutta 4 combines four slope estimates. Reduce the time step to see how the predicted paths move toward the exact linear-drag curve.

The quadratic-drag curve uses RK4 with a drag force proportional to speed squared. Both drag laws start with the same acceleration from drag.

Transform the equations of motion

Let u = x′ and w = y′ be horizontal and vertical velocity. For linear drag k and gravity g, the model is

u′ = −ku    w′ = −g − kw

Applying the derivative rule with initial velocities u₀, w₀ gives

U(s) = u₀/(s + k)
W(s) = w₀/(s + k) − g/[s(s + k)]
