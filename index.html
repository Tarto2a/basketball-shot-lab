What angle reaches the rim?

Keep the speed fixed and solve y(xrim; θ) − 3.05 = 0. Bisection and safeguarded Newton solve the same equation. The results include angles where the ball is moving down at the hoop.

The shot preview counts a descending ball within ±12 cm of rim height as in range. Rim and backboard collisions are outside this model.

How much does the time step matter?

Euler uses the slope at the start of each step. Runge–Kutta 4 combines four slope estimates. Reduce the time step to see how the predicted paths move toward the exact linear-drag curve.

The optional quadratic-drag curve uses RK4 and matches the linear drag acceleration at release.

Transform the equations of motion

Let u = x′ and w = y′ be horizontal and vertical velocity. For linear drag k and gravity g, the model is

u′ = −ku    w′ = −g − kw

Applying the derivative rule with initial velocities u₀, w₀ gives

U(s) = u₀/(s + k)
W(s) = w₀/(s + k) − g/[s(s + k)]
